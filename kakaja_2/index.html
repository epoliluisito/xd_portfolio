KAKAJA

a game of dice

Who is at the table?

You, plus one to three opponents.

Playing to

11,000 is the usual game.

How to play

Six dice. First to 11,000.

Your turn

Throw all six. Set aside the dice that score by tapping them, then either throw what's left or bank everything you've set aside. You must set aside at least one scoring die to carry on.

Throw nothing scoring and it's Tutto — every point from this turn is gone and play passes on. Get all six dice to score and the whole set comes back, points intact.

In the same throw, a fourth matching die doubles the three-of-a-kind, a fifth triples it and a sixth quadruples it — so six 1s are 4,000.

Winning

The round always finishes. Once someone passes the target, everyone left in that round must pass it and beat their total to take it — so reaching 11,000 first only wins if nobody after you gets to 11,001.
