Casting photos

How to take photos for biometric casting

Quick overview of process.

1. Set up

  • Face a window or soft daylight, so the light on you is even and shadows are soft or totally absent.
  • Stand in front of a plain wall - at least 50cm away if possible.
  • Hold the phone straight and steady. No filters, no zoom.

2. Face

  • Actors need to look straight at the camera.
  • Then they need to turn their head slowly: left, right, up, down.
  • Take a few with a neutral face, a smile, and your mouth open.
  • Do one set with hair off face.

3. Body

  • Fit the whole body in the frame, head to toe.
  • Take the front, the back, and each side.
  • Arms a little away from the body, hands showing.
  • Include detail of hands in the photos.
  • Actors should wear something plain and close-fitting.

4. Before you send them

  • Retake any that are blurry or dark.
  • The more photos, the better.

Final check

  • Even, soft light; plain wall, around 50cm back.
  • Phone straight and steady — no filters, no zoom.
  • Face straight on, then left, right, up and down.
  • Neutral, smile and mouth open; one set with hair off the face.
  • Whole body in frame: front, back and each side.
  • Arms slightly out, hands showing, with a few hand close-ups.
  • Plain, close-fitting clothes.
  • Retake anything blurry or dark. The more photos, the better.
