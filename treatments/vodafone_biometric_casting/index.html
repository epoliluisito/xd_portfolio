Casting photos

How to take your casting photos

A few minutes on your phone. Here's what to shoot.

1. Set up

  • Face a window or soft daylight, so the light on you is even and shadows are soft.
  • Stand in front of a plain wall.
  • Hold the phone straight and steady. No filters, no zoom.

2. Your face

  • Look straight at the camera.
  • Turn your head slowly: left, right, up, down.
  • Take a few with a neutral face, a smile, and your mouth open.
  • Do one set with your hair off your face.

3. Your body

  • Step back so your whole body fits, head to toe.
  • Take the front, the back, and each side.
  • Arms a little away from your body, hands showing.
  • Wear something plain and close-fitting.

4. Before you send them

  • Keep your whole head and feet in every shot — nothing cut off.
  • Retake any that are blurry or dark.
  • The more photos, the better.
