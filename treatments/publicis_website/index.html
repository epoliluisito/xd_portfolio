02 Magnum AI Flavour Remix
Source video → AI remix

Unlocking a new way to produce content, with more scalability than ever before.

AI asset extraction straight from the source video — a full 3D CGI model pulled out of finished footage, then re-lit and re-flavoured. Macro CGI detail where the product has to hold up close.

03 Seven Seas Three variants
Variant exploration

One idea, three executions — generated, graded and compared side by side.

The value here is speed of exploration: enough versions to actually choose between, tested before anything is committed to an edit.

04 Interstellar Character replacement
Likeness & continuity study

Two character replacements dropped into a famous scene.

An internal test of the hardest thing in the category: swapping a performer while the performance, the lighting and the grade all survive. Not for release — a measure of where the tooling actually is.

The line we hold

The audience does not reject AI. It rejects AI that forgets the audience.

AI that works
When it serves the story, the craft and the audience.
Supports the idea
Used to explore, extend and strengthen the creative vision.
Enhances craft
A tool in the hands of skilled creators — not a replacement.
Solves a real problem
Saves time, reduces cost, or unlocks something that wasn't possible.
Is transparent
Disclosed, consented, and rights respected.
AI that backfires
When it undermines trust, quality and connection.
Feels cheap
The output looks generic, soulless or low quality.
Lacks authenticity
Performances without depth or emotion.
Misuses likeness
Identities, voices or performances used without consent.
Gets facts wrong
Incorrect claims, fake data or misleading content.
