The Workspace

We build the campaign as a single shared workspace — an open board where the director leads and the creative team stays alongside, from the first option to the final master. Alignment and iteration happen in the same place the work is made, not in a chain of decks and mails.

The Artists

The craft sits
in between.

An AI artist is not someone who writes prompts. They read a frame like a cinematographer and build it like an engineer — holding lens, light and continuity in one hand, and models, references and conditioning in the other.

From the filmmaker
  • Lens & light — why a frame reads
  • Blocking & performance — why it moves
  • Continuity — why it holds across shots
  • The cut — why it lands
The AI ArtistBoth, at once
From the engineer
  • which model, and why — Model selection
  • how a likeness survives — Reference & conditioning
  • a hundred, not one — Iteration at volume
  • what carries downstream — The pipeline
They speak to the creative team in one language and to the model in another, and nothing is lost in between. It is the difference between a generated film and a film.
