The Approach

Six stages,
one connected flow.

Every stage feeds the next. Casting informs the frame. The frame becomes the film. Nothing is re-made — it is carried forward.

Applied

Twenty-five films.
Twenty-five castings.

Three castings and three locations for every film — shortlisted, art-directed, and put in front of you. You align on one. The pipeline re-forms around that decision: the style frame, the ratios, the clean files, the AE package.

The Workspace

One canvas.
The whole production.

We build the campaign as a single shared workspace — an open board where the director leads and the creative team stays alongside, from the first option to the final master. Alignment and iteration happen in the same place the work is made, not in a chain of decks and mails.
