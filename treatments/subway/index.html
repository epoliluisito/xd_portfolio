The Brief

Test AI on real C4 assets — find where it creates meaningful efficiencies.

Without compromising food craft, brand consistency or approval confidence. Every test starts from approved Subway photography and proves whether the workflow can repeat across future LTOs.

What We Test

The AI enhancement layer.

Stills → Motion Primary

Approved photography adapted into simple motion — camera moves, pans, zooms, sauce movement — no separate film shoot.

Asset Adaptation Secondary

One master asset versioned across 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 and beyond, holding brand consistency.

Background Generation Secondary

Product extractions placed into new AI environments — seasonal, regional, format-specific.

Ingredient Generation Secondary

Ingredients generated for customised subs and falling B-roll, keeping the original look and feel.

AI Voiceover Secondary

Synthetic VO for realistic, human-like delivery — comedic timing and tone in line with human VO.
