The brief, in short

We're replicating the Shower Fresh genematic that was created pre-shoot, but with new talent playing the hero and her friend. P&G requires that the new talent be real people, cast and credited, whose likeness we recreate in AI with their consent.

This isn't a simple face-swap. We're regenerating the scene from scratch with the new characters, which gives us the freedom to keep the visual storytelling intention intact while opening up new creative exploration — but it also means most of the six production steps are run again, not skipped. The one thing we carry over from the original genematic is the established visual workflow: location, shot logic and style are already proven, which is what keeps this faster than starting a new production cold.

Bottom line: lock talent and consent in week 1 and we're realistically looking at delivery in week 5–6. If talent sourcing slips, the whole timeline slips with it — day for day.
