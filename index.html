MERCEDES ITALIA 150TH ANNIVERSARY·event direction & spatial design

Bologna's historic streets turned into a synchronized brand tribute
Logistical Choreography
A dynamic car parade through active city traffic, brand legacy in focus
The Visual Climax
A final showcase engineered as an emotional crescendo honoring Mercedes' Italian roots
